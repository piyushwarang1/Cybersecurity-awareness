Cybersecurity Awareness

Cyber threats aren't always complicated

Many attacks begin with a simple message, a weak password, or a moment of carelessness.

Understanding how common threats work is the first step toward protecting yourself.

PHISHING

Fake emails, messages, or websites designed to trick you into giving away information.

MALWARE

Malicious software that is designed to harm or exploit any programmable device or network.

SOCIAL ENGINEERING

The use of psychological manipulation to deceive individuals into revealing confidential information.

RANSOMWARE

A type of malware that encrypts a victim's files and demands payment to restore access.

01/Phishing

Phishing attempts often try to make you act before you have time to think.

1

UNEXPECTED MESSAGE

You receive an unexpected email or message that seems legitimate but contains suspicious links or attachments.

2

URGENT DEMAND

The message creates a sense of urgency, demanding immediate action to avoid negative consequences.

3

SUSPICIOUS LINKS

The message contains links that lead to fake websites designed to steal your information.

4

REQUEST FOR INFORMATION

The message asks for sensitive information like passwords or credit card details.

5

FAKE WEBSITES

The message contains links that lead to fake websites designed to steal your information.

6

CHECK THE SENDER

Always verify the sender's email address or phone number to ensure it's legitimate.

02/Malware

Malware is software designed to harm your device or steal your information.

1

VIRUSES

Programs that can replicate themselves and spread to other devices, often causing damage.

2

WORMS

Self-replicating malware that spreads across networks without user intervention.

3

TROJANS

Malware disguised as legitimate software, tricking users into installing it.

4

SPYWARE

Software that secretly monitors your activities and collects personal information.

5

ADWARE

Software that displays unwanted advertisements, often bundled with free programs.

6

RANSOMWARE

A type of malware that encrypts your files and demands payment for their release.

03/Social Engineering

Social engineering is the art of manipulating people into revealing confidential information or performing actions that compromise security.

1

PRETEXTING

Creating a fabricated scenario to engage a victim and gain their trust.

2

BULLYING

Using intimidation or threats to pressure someone into revealing information.

3

TRUST BUILDING

Establishing a relationship with a victim to gain their confidence.

4

EMOTIONAL manipulation

Using emotions to influence a victim's behavior and decision-making.

5

PHYSICAL ACCESS

Gaining physical access to a location or device to steal information.
