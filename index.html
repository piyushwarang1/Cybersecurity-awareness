Cybersecurity Awareness

THREATS ARE EVERYWHERE!

PHISHING

Fake emails, messages, or websites designed to trick you into giving away information.

MALWARE

Malicious software that is designed to harm or exploit any programmable device or network.

SOCIAL ENGINEERING

The use of psychological manipulation to deceive individuals into revealing confidential information.

YOUR DIGITAL FOOTPRINT

WHAT IS IT?

Your digital footprint is the trail of data you leave behind when you use the internet.

HOW TO PROTECT IT?

Be mindful of what you share online, use strong passwords, and regularly review your privacy settings.

WHY IS IT IMPORTANT?

Your digital footprint can affect your personal and professional life, so it's important to manage it carefully.
