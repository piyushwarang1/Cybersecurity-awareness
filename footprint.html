01 / Types of Footprint

Active Footprint

knowingly and intentionally share online. Examples include posting on social media, uploading photos, filling out web forms, and writing reviews.

Passive Footprint

automatically and often unknowingly in the background as you browse or use apps. Examples include your IP address, device type, search history, location tracking, and website cookies

03 / Why it Matters?

Privacy & Security

Large footprints can expose you to data tracking, targeted ads, phishing scams, or identity theft.

Reputation & Careers

Schools, universities, and potential employers often review public online activity during admissions or job screenings.

Permanence

Information posted online can be copied, shared, or archived, making it difficult to erase completely
